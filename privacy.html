Privacy Policy

PyPath: Learn Python · Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version: PyPath does not collect any data about you.

What PyPath stores

PyPath saves your lesson progress, your challenge code and the programs you write in the Playground, plus your editor settings. All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Files that your own Python programs create are also kept in the app's folder on your device.

What PyPath does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own. It works fully offline.

Code you run

PyPath runs the Python code you type, on your device. Python can reach the internet if your own program asks it to (for example with urllib); PyPath never does this by itself, and nothing you write is sent to us.

Sharing

If you use the Share button on a Playground program, iOS shares that program's text with the app you choose. That's up to you, and we never see it.

Children

PyPath collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
